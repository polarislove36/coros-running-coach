# Strength Training for Runners

Use this reference when a running plan includes strength training. Strength rows in a plan must tell the user exactly what to do: exercise names, sets, reps or duration, and intensity guidance. Do not write only `力量训练 30分钟`.

## Coaching Principles

- Strength work supports running economy, durability, posture, hip control, and late-race form. It should not create soreness that compromises key runs.
- For most recreational road runners, use 1-2 strength sessions per week.
- In base and build phases, use two categories: **核心力量** and **腿部力量**. Add calf/foot work inside leg strength.
- Put strength on a non-long-run day. Prefer after an easy run or on a separate day. Avoid heavy leg strength within 24-36 hours before a long run or quality workout.
- Keep at least 1-2 reps in reserve on most sets. Stop any movement that creates unusual muscle soreness, obvious weakness, sharp pain, or next-day soreness that makes running feel abnormal.
- Race week or taper: remove heavy lower-body work; keep only light activation, core, and calf/foot maintenance if needed.

## Weekly Default

If the user agrees to strength training:

- **1 session/week**: use Session A, full-body runner strength.
- **2 sessions/week**: use Session A and Session B, separated by at least 48 hours when possible.
- **Beginner or busy week**: 20-25 minutes, 2 sets per exercise, bodyweight or light resistance.
- **Experienced/gym available**: 35-45 minutes, 2-4 sets, moderate-to-heavy but technically clean.

## Session A: Core + Single-Leg Strength

Use in base weeks and most normal training weeks.

| Block | Exercise | Dose | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Warm-up | Glute bridge | 2 x 10-12 | Pause 1 second at the top |
| Warm-up | Mini-band lateral walk or monster walk | 2 x 10-12 steps each side | Keep pelvis level |
| Core | Dead bug | 2-3 x 8-10 each side | Low back stays quiet |
| Core | Side plank | 2-3 x 25-45 sec each side | Progress by lifting top leg |
| Leg | Split squat | 2-3 x 8-10 each side | Bodyweight first, then dumbbells |
| Leg | Romanian deadlift or single-leg RDL | 2-3 x 8-10 | Hinge from hips, neutral back |
| Calf/foot | Standing calf raise | 2-3 x 12-15 | Slow lower, full range |

Short version: dead bug, side plank, split squat, RDL, calf raise.

## Session B: Posterior Chain + Trunk Control

Use as the second weekly strength session when the user can recover from it.

| Block | Exercise | Dose | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Warm-up | Clamshell or banded hip external rotation | 2 x 12 each side | Feel glute med, not low back |
| Core | Front plank or dynamic plank | 2-3 x 30-45 sec | Stop before form sags |
| Core | Bird dog or Pallof press | 2-3 x 8-12 each side | Anti-rotation control |
| Leg | Step-up or bench step-over | 2-3 x 8-10 each side | Drive through whole foot |
| Leg | Walking lunge | 2-3 x 8-10 each side | Smooth, stable knee tracking |
| Posterior chain | Hip thrust or glute bridge march | 2-3 x 8-12 | Glutes, not low back |
| Calf/foot | Bent-knee calf raise or soleus raise | 2-3 x 12-15 | Important for distance runners |

Short version: plank, Pallof press/bird dog, step-up, lunge, bent-knee calf raise.

## Gym Progression

Only use heavier gym work when the runner already tolerates basic strength training.

- Weeks 1-2: technique and tissue adaptation, 2 sets of 8-12, RPE 5-6.
- Weeks 3-6: build, 2-3 sets of 6-10, RPE 6-7.
- Weeks 7+: experienced runners may use 3-4 sets of 4-8 on split squat, squat, deadlift/RDL, or step-up, RPE 7-8, while keeping run workouts high quality.
- Do not chase 1RM during a running block. Use clean reps and repeatability.

## Plyometric Add-On

Do not add plyometrics in the first week of a base phase for recreational runners. Add only after several weeks of pain-free running and strength consistency.

Beginner add-on, once ready:

- Pogo hops: 2 x 15-20 seconds.
- Skipping or ankling drills: 2 x 20-30 meters.
- Low box step-off to stick landing: 2 x 5 each side.

Rules:

- Keep contacts low at first.
- Do plyometrics before strength or after an easy warm-up, not after a fatiguing long run.
- Avoid with Achilles, calf, plantar fascia, knee, or bone-stress symptoms.

## How to Write Strength Rows in a Plan

Bad:

- `力量训练 30-40分钟`

Good:

- `力量训练A（核心+单腿）：臀桥 2x12；死虫 3x8/侧；侧桥 2x30秒/侧；分腿蹲 3x8/侧；罗马尼亚硬拉 3x8；站姿提踵 3x12。强度 RPE 6-7，不练到酸痛。`

For a recovery week:

- `轻力量维护：死虫 2x8/侧；侧桥 2x25秒/侧；徒手分腿蹲 2x8/侧；提踵 2x12。全程轻松，不追重量。`

For race week:

- `激活：臀桥 2x10；死虫 2x6/侧；提踵 2x10；弹力带侧走 2x8步/侧。全程轻松，不做重腿。`

## Source Notes

- Strength-training reviews in middle- and long-distance runners report improvements in running economy and performance-related determinants when heavy resistance, plyometric, or combined methods are integrated without disrupting running.
- NSCA runner-strength examples emphasize multi-joint movements, single-leg patterns, core/spinal control, hip/glute activation, and reduced strength volume during peaking/taper periods.
